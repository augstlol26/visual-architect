# Visual identity

## Components
Personality and emotional register · colour system · typography roles · shape language · surface language · iconography and illustration · photography treatment · motion character · voice and microcopy.

## Identity brief
Capture: three to five personality descriptors and their opposites ("confident, not loud"); the intended emotional response; references and *what specifically* is admired in each; colour feelings and constraints; typography character; imagery direction; surface and texture; patterns to avoid; accessibility and legibility needs.

## Typeface pairing strategies

| Strategy | Effect | Example roles |
|---|---|---|
| **Serif display + sans body** | Editorial authority with modern readability | Literary, cultural, premium content |
| **Sans display + serif body** | Contemporary headline, bookish reading | Long-form journalism, essays |
| **Grotesk + mono** | Technical, precise | Dev tools, data, engineering |
| **Single superfamily** | Calm, systematic | Product UI, documentation |
| **Expressive display + neutral body** | Personality carried by headlines only | Brands, events, campaigns |
| **Condensed display + wide body** | Poster energy, high information density | Sport, news, music |

Choose by voice, then check: x-height and legibility at body size, character set and language support, variable axes available, licence, and loading cost.

## Palette construction

| Method | Use when |
|---|---|
| **Domain-derived** (sample the subject's real materials) | Almost always the most ownable starting point |
| **Neutral-led + one accent** | Content or imagery should dominate |
| **Monochrome ramp** | Strong typography carries the identity |
| **Analogous** | Calm, cohesive, organic moods |
| **Complementary accent** | You need an unmistakable call to action |
| **High-contrast duo** | Bold, poster-like brands |

Build each colour as a ramp (about 50–950), assign semantic roles from the ramp, then verify contrast for every text pair. Tint neutrals slightly toward the accent so the palette feels intended.

## Shape and surface
Radius scale (sharp, soft, pill) chosen as a personality signal; borders vs shadows as the main separator; texture (grain, paper, noise) at low opacity; elevation levels kept to three or four.

## Terminology
**Hue** colour family · **Value** lightness · **Saturation** intensity · **Tint/shade** mixed with white/black · **Elevation** perceived layer · **Type scale** coordinated sizes · **Variable font** adjustable axes such as weight or width.

Colours, fonts, and brand assets become `Confirmed by user` only on the user's explicit confirmation.
